Takeaways:

* Neither program refused a good start. PX4 left Offboard because the RC sticks moved:
  keep the throttle stick still after switching to Position. The override parameters are
  the organisers' (`contracts/px4.md`), not ours to change.
* traj stops for good once Offboard has started and ended (`mission.py` `_update`), so a
  second arm on the same launch cannot fly: restart the competitor window first.
* cuVSLAM jumps metres during a manual landing and while the drone is handled on the
  ground, and logs frame gaps of 66–166 ms (threshold 40 ms) throughout. Cause not found.
