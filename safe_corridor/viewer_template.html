How it works

Input: a CAD mesh and a rough route of named openings. Frames: each opening's outline is measured from the mesh. Tube: each frame's outline blends straight into the next, so halfway its top and bottom are the averages of the two frames'; walls only trim it. Line: pulled taut through each frame's centre, pushed off a wall only where the drone (a 0.32 × 0.32 × 0.12 m ellipsoid) would have under 25 cm of margin.
